Mini Jame Gam #16

Theme: Eat
Special Object: Bag

2D, pixel art graphics, platformer / top down navigation?
Setting could be a grocery store (to use the bag object to collect food)
Are we collecting food for ourself or for others in need? 

Maybe its a dark dystopian atmosphere, everyone is on the brink of starvation, 
we travel and meet new people, taking a bag with us to collect artifacts from them.

Yeah lets go with that, this can just be a 2d platformer with a dark color scheme for 90% of the game,


Scope: 
1 tutorial level (how to move, interact, world building)
3 main levels (Longer platforming levels increasing in difficulty as the player progresses, 
    each presents the player with a new NPC giving them an artifact to help them along their journey)
1 Boss Level (The player faces the authoritarian regime and fights their way to freedom, this could be a type of parkour bullet hell sequence)
    Defeating the boss requires (or suggests the use of) all retrieved artifacts from the NPCs along the way.
    In the end, the player suffocates the dictator with the bag they have had this entire time. Once the dictator is slain, the world is free at last.
